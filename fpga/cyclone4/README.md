# Cyclone IV reference projects

These board-specific Quartus projects are retained as a separate reference.
They are not part of the DE10-Lite/MAX 10 implementation or the hardware
comparison reported in the paper.

The active article targets and reproduction instructions are documented in
[`fpga/de10_lite/README.md`](../de10_lite/README.md) and
[`docs/reproduction.md`](../../docs/reproduction.md). Do not transfer pin
assignments, device constraints, or resource results between the two FPGA
families without a separate build and timing review.
